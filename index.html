How it works

GPS gives a position when it has a view of the sky. Without it, the app counts steps and follows the compass from your last known point. The error grows by about 5 % of the distance walked. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth positioning compare nearby signals with a database: your own saved fingerprints, known beacon coordinates, or the online BeaconDB. They need the Android app; browsers cannot scan Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

Experimental prototype — not for emergency navigation.
