How it works

GPS gives a position when it has a view of the sky. Without it, the app counts steps and follows the compass from your last known point. The error grows by about 5 % of the distance walked. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth positioning need a native Android app and are not available in a browser.

Experimental prototype — not for emergency navigation.
